Transformers are widely used in many AI systems. Understanding how they internally works is essential for building and improving these models.

Transformers consist of 3 main parts:
* Word embedding
Takes words/images and converts them into vectors
* Positional encoding 
helps keep track of the word order
* Attention
Helps transformers by establishing relationship among words/image patches

In this work, I am implementing toy examples to better understand the essence of these parts.
